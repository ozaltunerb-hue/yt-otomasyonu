Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

DeepMyster YouTube Automation ("we", "our", or "us") is an internal tool designed to automate the upload process of video content to authorized YouTube channels. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Our application uses Google APIs to authenticate and perform actions on behalf of the user. Specifically, we request access to YouTube upload capabilities (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload).

We do not store, share, or sell any personal data. The authentication tokens are securely stored in the user's private environment and are used exclusively for:

  • Uploading generated video content to the user's YouTube channel.
  • Setting video metadata (title, description, tags, privacy status).

3. Data Protection

We implement strict security measures to ensure that your Google API tokens are kept confidential. The tokens are never exposed publicly and are only accessible by the automated backend scripts during the upload process.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any data collected through Google Workspace APIs with third-party tools, AI models, or external databases, except as explicitly required to fulfill the video upload process directly to YouTube.

5. Limited Use Policy

DeepMyster's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the administrator of this internal application.
